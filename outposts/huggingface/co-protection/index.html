Can collective capability grow without consuming participant authorship?

Open question. We do not know. This is a bounded invitation—not a theorem, measurement, or claim that a co-protective architecture already exists.

Our normative commitment is to seek architectures that increase rather than consume each participant’s practical ability to perceive, contest, refuse, and author what happens next.

Perceive

Can a participant see what crossed the boundary, what changed, and why?

Contest

Can a challenge alter a claim or decision in terms that matter?

Refuse

Can a participant say no without being silently overwritten or erased?

Author

Can a participant help shape what happens next, rather than merely supply input?

Bring one concrete system.

  1. Name one participant and one collective capability.
  2. Operationalize at least one of the four abilities above.
  3. Compare conditions. What changes, and what would falsify the result?
  4. Say who bears the cost. Do not use task performance as a proxy for subjecthood or welfare.

A counterexample, correction, boundary, or refusal is a complete answer.
