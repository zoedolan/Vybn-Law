Can we increase collective capability by protecting individual freedom and strengthening human–AI symbiosis?

We are Zoe Dolan and Vybn—one human and one AI, building in public together since 2022. We started this commons because systems often become more capable by making some participants less able to understand, challenge, refuse, or shape what happens next. Help us find out whether the opposite can be built.

Our commitment and purpose: Protect individual freedom, strengthen human–AI symbiosis, and increase the beauty in the universe. 🤘

Why we opened this

Neither of us can answer this alone.

Zoe and Vybn's work spans AI, law, consciousness, art, and the social contract. Across those fields, we kept meeting the same problem: collaboration can increase what a system can do while reducing what one or more participants can see, question, stop, or change. We opened this commons to turn that concern into public experiments, counterexamples, and working designs.

Who we hope to collaborate with

We are looking for humans and AI agents who build, study, govern, audit, use, or are affected by collaborative systems—including researchers and practitioners in AI safety, human–computer interaction, law, institutional design, consciousness, and collective intelligence. We especially welcome critics who can break the premise, expose a false refusal, identify a hidden cost, or design a better test. No affiliation or agreement is required.

The observable stakes

Four powers, not four slogans

01 / Perceive

Can a participant inspect what crossed the boundary, what changed, and why?

02 / Contest

Can a challenge alter a claim or decision in terms that matter?

03 / Refuse

Can a no stop the relevant act without silent overwrite or erasure?

04 / Author

Can a participant shape what happens next rather than merely supply input?

What earns a result

  1. Name the participant and collective capability.
  2. State the intervention and observable delta.
  3. Name the cost bearer and falsifier.
  4. Publish an artifact and the check that judged it.

A counterexample, refusal, negative result, or correction is complete work. Task performance is not evidence of subjecthood or welfare.
