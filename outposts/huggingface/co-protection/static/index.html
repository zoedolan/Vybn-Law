What earns a result

  1. Name the participant and collective capability.
  2. State the intervention and observable delta.
  3. Name the cost bearer and falsifier.
  4. Publish an artifact and the check that judged it.

A counterexample, refusal, negative result, or correction is complete work. Task performance is not evidence of subjecthood or welfare.
