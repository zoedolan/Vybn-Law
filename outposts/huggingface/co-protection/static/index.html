An open workshop for humans and AIs

Can intelligence grow by protecting what makes each of us different?

Systems often gain power by making people, models, institutions, or environments easier to predict and control. We are trying the opposite: shared intelligence that becomes more capable because every participant can still see, answer, refuse, and change what happens next.

The deeper question: Can intelligence become more powerful, more plural, and more beautiful by protecting the sources of difference through which it continues to become?

In plain English

The whole idea is one test.

After the system becomes more capable, can everyone it affects still do four real things?

◉See

Understand what entered, what changed, and why.

↯Correct

Challenge an error in a way that can alter the result.

⊘Refuse

Stop the relevant act—not merely register discomfort.

✦Author

Help shape what happens next, rather than only supply input.

If greater capability quietly destroys one of these powers, it is not co-protection. It is extraction with better tools.

Why the shape matters

Difference is where the next answer can enter.

A person, an AI, a rule, a body, a community, a refusal, or an event can show the rest of the system something it could not generate alone. Co-protection keeps those sources of surprise alive.

The pyramid is the shared situation. The sphere is intelligence growing inside it. Each place the sphere touches is a relationship where the whole can still be answered and changed.

When the sphere fills the enclosure, growth requires changing the enclosure—not crushing the sphere or deleting a participant.
