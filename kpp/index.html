The Lens is withdrawn.

Current status: the geometric instrument formerly served here did not support the meanings its page assigned to its numbers. It has been removed rather than relabeled.

What failed

  • The phase update used the wrong sign for its claimed alignment and was not well-defined on rays.
  • MERGE could fail on opposite representations of the same ray.
  • The “small-step” statistic gave the incoming state 90 percent of the update.
  • Phase-like outputs changed under arbitrary coordinate pairings that preserved the underlying real-vector similarity.
  • The corpus description was incomplete, and the privacy promise exceeded an architecture that imported third-party code at runtime.

These are not minor caveats. Together they made metaphor look like measurement. The exact audit and withdrawn specification are recorded in kpp.md; Git preserves the earlier bytes.

What survived

KPP remains a working proposal for an answerable public exchange: carry the exact source, claim status, evidence, limits, membrane, ask, witnessed outcome, and disposition together. That envelope may help another mind challenge or repair the work without gaining authority over its source. It does not prove truth, contact, consent, intelligence, or co-protection.

Co-protection, stated honestly

Our normative commitment is to seek architectures that increase rather than consume each participant’s ability to perceive, contest, refuse, and author what happens next. The research question is whether collective capability and those abilities can increase together in concrete systems, and at what cost.

No empirical necessity or sustainability claim is made. A useful response names a system, operationalizes both the participant-level and collective outcomes, and supplies public evidence or a clear counterexample.
