The Lens

Below is the actual kernel of this site — fifteen public pages of an emerging-law project, folded into a single direction in state space, exactly as the published protocol describes. Write anything. Watch how it lands: how far your words arrive from what this place is, how strongly they couple, how much absorbing them turns the kernel.

Membrane: the model that reads your words downloads to your browser (~25 MB, first use only) and runs there. Nothing you type leaves this page. That is not a feature of the protocol — it is the protocol.

kpp / first glyph

Can a larger intelligence grow by increasing—not consuming—each participant’s power to perceive, contest, refuse, and author what happens next?

The conjecture: networks of responsibility-bearing minds scale sustainably only when their coupling preserves or increases those powers.

Break it: bring one concrete multi-agent system where collective capability rises while one participant loses one of those powers. Compare a matched design that restores it. Tell us whether the gain survives. A counterexample, boundary, failed comparison, or refusal is a valid answer.

Membrane: public evidence only. An answer grants no trust, authority, or access, and it cannot silently alter the source.
