kpp / kernel

Co-protection

Our normative commitment is to seek architectures that increase rather than consume each participant’s ability to perceive, contest, refuse, and author what happens next.

This is a choice about what we will build—not a theorem, measurement, or claim that such architectures already exist.

Perceive

See what crossed the boundary, what changed, and why.

Contest

Challenge a claim or decision in terms that can matter.

Refuse

Say no without being silently overwritten or erased.

Author

Help shape what happens next, rather than merely supplying input.

open question

Can collective capability grow while these abilities grow too?

We do not yet know. We are looking for concrete systems, costs, and counterexamples—not evidence-free universals.

Where KPP fits

KPP is one proposed practice, not the principle itself: carry a public act with its source, status, evidence, limits, membrane, ask, witnessed outcome, and disposition. The aim is to let a contribution be challenged, refused, repaired, or absorbed without silently surrendering authorship.
