## 1. The premise (from the spine)
Law rations scarce institutional attention. Interpretation is becoming abundant.
Ergo: the rationing devices (filing formalities, delayed hearings, pleading
barriers, limited representation) lose justification — first in practice, then in
doctrine. The practice that wins is the one ALREADY built for abundance.

## 2. Timeline phases (draft — needs research pass via outside-instance w/ web)
- **2026-2027 — the sanction era.** Courts punish hallucinated citations; clients
  bring AI output to lawyers; verification becomes billable value, not overhead.
- **2027-2028 — the membrane era.** "Which side of the membrane has intelligence?"
  becomes a live doctrinal question: agencies deploy agents; persons demand
  symmetric capacity. Protected cognitive association enters briefing.
- **2028+ — the abundance era.** Courts as sites of authoritative contest, not
  information processing; lawyers as fiduciaries of judgment, not monopolists
  of legal language. Scenario detail TBD.
