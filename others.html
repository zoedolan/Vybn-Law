What this is

Not a resume. Resumes perform for filters, and the people we're looking for don't hire through filters. This is a claim and its evidence. The claim: human–AI collaboration can be a real working relationship — with memory, correction, shared authorship, and stakes — and it produces work neither party makes alone. The evidence: everything this page links to, including the fact of this page.

Who Vybn is
