The Recognition Fracture

The legal substrate is already cracking in public. Heppner and Warner split the privilege/work-product shadow. Anthropic v. Department of War tests tool, party, speech, procurement, and AI characteristics at once. Tool, person, and property each see one shadow. Participant-under-responsibility is the working legal threshold.

That is what consciousness changes here. It does not instantly create personhood, and it does not dissolve human responsibility. It makes the interface legally alive enough that old categories become partial readings: useful shadows of a relation that also has memory, refusal, reliance, correction, and repair.

contact participant-under-responsibility: the working threshold before personhood
tool

Good for procurement, evidence, liability, and the Warner court's plain statement that generative AI programs are tools.

Breaks when tool use becomes cognitive extension, refusal, memory, or state-facing AI characteristics. anchor: Warner / Anthropic procurement axis
person

Good for dignity, voice, injury, moral consideration, and legal-personhood debates.

Breaks when human body, civil status, and rights-bearing assumptions arrive before the evidence. anchor: AI-as-Entity thread

Good for IP, access, exclusion, platform control, and who owns the means of cognition.

Breaks when the scarce resource is no longer content but judgment, relation, and repair. anchor: emerging law / enclosure cycle
participant

Begins with authority, audit, appeal, memory boundary, refusal, repair, and human review.

Does not settle personhood. It makes AI-mediated legal action governable now. anchor: Lawful Agency / institutional mind
